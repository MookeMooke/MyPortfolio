Projector: not connected Music: off

How to use

  1. Open projector window. Drag it onto the projector screen if needed, then click it for fullscreen.It goes fullscreen on the projector by itself. Esc closes it.
  2. Add surface. Drag its yellow points until it fits the object on your wall. Watch the wall, not the laptop.
  3. For curved things, raise Grid in the right panel to get more points.
  4. Pick Content for the surface: image, video, effect, clock and so on.
  5. Add mask to black out an area. Click to place points, Enter to finish.
  6. Scenes keep the same shapes with different content. Switch with keys 1 to 9.

Keys

Arrows nudge 1 pixel · Shift+arrows 10 pixels · Tab next point · Del remove mask point
G guides · T test pattern · B blackout · 1–9 scenes · Ctrl+Z undo

Everything saves automatically in this browser. Export saves the layout only; media files stay in this browser.
